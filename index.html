Oliver OS

Oliver OS is a personal dashboard and daily brief that Oliver Cowin built for his own use.

It runs only on his own laptop. It reads his own calendar and email with read-only access, so it can show what's due today and what's slipping. It never sends, changes, or deletes anything.

It isn't offered to anyone else, and there's no account to sign up for.
