Oliver OS Privacy Policy

Last updated October 21, 2026

Oliver OS is a personal dashboard that Oliver Cowin built for his own use. It runs only on his own laptop and isn't offered to anyone else.

What it reads

  • Google Calendar, read-only: event titles, times, and locations from his own calendars.
  • Gmail, read-only (once connected): senders, subject lines, dates, and short snippets from his own inboxes.

It never sends, changes, or deletes any email or calendar event.

Where the data goes

Everything is saved only on the laptop where Oliver OS runs. There is no Oliver OS server or cloud account. Data is never sold, shared, or used for advertising.

To write a short daily summary, Oliver OS sends the minimum needed (titles, dates, senders, subject lines, and short snippets, never full email bodies) to Anthropic's Claude API, which writes the summary and sends it back.

Google user data

Oliver OS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Turning it off

Access can be removed at any time from Google Account settings: Security, then Third-party apps and services, then Oliver OS, then Remove access.
